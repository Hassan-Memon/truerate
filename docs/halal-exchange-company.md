# Idea: a fee-only exchange company with zero spread

Notes from 6 November 2026. **Status: idea recorded, paused the same day.** This page is not legal or religious advice. The fiqh section is a summary of published sources, gathered by an AI assistant and not yet reviewed by a scholar. A written fatwa and a reading of the current State Bank rules are needed before any step.

## 1. The idea, in the founder's words

A money-changing company that buys and sells at the exact rate of each currency, with no gain at all from the exchange itself. It earns only a service charge that the customer can see, plus other halal income from its knowledge of the market. It starts in Karachi and grows worldwide, and it follows Hanafi fiqh as a halal business.

## 2. The model: one price, one fee

Buy and sell each currency at the same single rate. Charge a separate fee, printed on the receipt.

There is a precedent online. Wise shows the mid-market rate and charges an explicit fee. In the year to March 2026 it moved $243.5bn and kept about 0.52% of that as fees ([Wise FY2026 results](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/wise-class-a--wise/wise-fy26-results/9637901)). No cash money changer using this model was found.

## 3. Three hard problems

**1. "Exact rate" needs a definition.** On 4 Nov 2026, interbank was 276.70, but cash dollars traded at 277.35 buy and 278.48 sell (see [`research.md`](research.md)). If the company sold cash at 276.70, buyers would empty the counter and no seller would come. So the exact rate should be one published rule, for example the open-market midpoint (277.915), printed with its time on every receipt.

**2. The margin being replaced is thin on paper.** The published spread is Rs 1.13, about 0.41%, so about 0.2% per side. That is Rs 565 on $1,000. The fee must be below that to win on price. So the real advantage lies elsewhere:

- customers often do not get the published rate (rates are negotiated, receipts are rare);
- crisis periods, when the gap widens (8.3% in September 2023);
- trust, which brings volume.

**3. Risk does not disappear.** The spread normally pays for holding dollars overnight while the price moves. The answer is to match buyers with sellers, hold little stock, and sell the surplus the same day. Early TransferWise did this and claimed matches on at least 60% of its volume by 2015 ([CNBC, 2015](https://www.cnbc.com/2015/05/12/transferwise-disruptor-50.html)).

## 4. Fiqh notes

| Point | What the sources say | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Profit on the rate | Different currencies may be exchanged with an excess on one side. So zero spread is the founder's higher standard of honesty, not a fiqh requirement. The company must never advertise that a competitor's spread is haram. | [SeekersGuidance](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/what-is-the-ruling-on-the-exchange-of-different-currencies/) |
| Possession | Hanafi scholars, as relayed from Mufti Taqi Usmani, require at least one side to take possession in the sitting. AAOIFI requires both sides. The safer design follows the stricter rule: both, hand to hand. | [Askimam](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/125576/currency-exchange-2/), [AAOIFI SS 1](https://islamicmarkets.com/standards/aaoifi-shariah-standards/trading-in-currencies/scope-shariah-ruling) |
| Forwards and futures | AAOIFI prohibits them. | AAOIFI SS 1 |
| The fee | It should be separate, known in advance, for a real service, and not cut from the exchanged money. Most sources found discuss same-currency exchange, so the mufti should rule on a fee added to a different-currency exchange, and on a flat fee against a percentage. | [Sinar Daily](https://www.sinardaily.my/article/734534/culture/islamic/mufti-warns-against-riba-in-duit-raya-banknote-exchange) |
| Capital | Fund with equity, not interest loans, and keep balances in Islamic accounts. This is an inference from the ban on riba, not from a source. | |

## 5. Law in Pakistan

- An exchange company needs a State Bank licence and Rs 500m of paid-up capital, about $1.8m, since September 2023 ([ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2023/09/06/sbp-raises-minimum-capital-requirement-for-exchange-companies-to-rs-500-million/)).
- Franchises and the small Category B licence were abolished in the same reform ([APP](https://www.app.com.pk/?p=681577)). There is no small legal entry.
- A new State Bank framework replaced the old manual from 1 January 2025. **It has not been read.** Whether a separate service fee is allowed is unconfirmed.

## 6. Other halal income

All are fees for a service, not gain on a rate:

- commission for paying out inward remittances as an agent;
- the TrueRate data API for businesses;
- currency service for small importers and for Hajj and Umrah travellers;
- a "TrueRate certified" audit for other money changers.

## 7. Path from Karachi to the world

| Stage | Work |
|---|---|
| 1 | The TrueRate site publishes the reference rate and the rule. Every receipt points to it. |
| 2 | A fatwa from a Karachi Hanafi institution, and a reading of the State Bank's 2025 framework |
| 3 | One pilot counter in Karachi under a licensed partner |
| 4 | Own licence, more cities |
| 5 | Saudi and UAE corridors, each with its own licence, then digital |

## 8. The business inside each risk

| Risk | Business point inside it |
|---|---|
| "Exact rate" has no definition | Whoever publishes the rule owns the benchmark. A "TrueRate Karachi cash rate" quoted by the media, shops and other changers is a brand, and later a paid data feed. |
| Thin margin (about 0.2% per side) | A wall against copying. A changer who lives on a hidden spread cannot match the model without losing income. |
| Customers do not get the published rate; no receipts | The receipt becomes the product: printed rate, time, fee and a QR code that checks it on TrueRate. The State Bank already treats non-system receipts as a complaint case. |
| Price risk from holding stock overnight | Matching is a business in itself. Customers book in an app, buyers are paired with sellers, and the counter only settles. The company also sees real demand and supply in Karachi's cash market. |
| Rs 500m licence capital | The barrier also protects. Licensed companies have the licence but lack trust and technology, so the rate engine, receipts and certification can be sold to them without owning a licence. |
| Strict fiqh rules | A fatwa and a yearly Shariah audit are a trust label. It opens Hajj and Umrah travellers, then the Gulf, Malaysia, Indonesia and Muslim communities in the UK. |
| Crisis, when the gap jumps | That is when people search hardest for an honest counter. A public daily gap index makes TrueRate the name they find. |

## 9. Two points that need care

- **A flat fee helps large customers and hurts small ones.** At today's spread a changer keeps about Rs 56 on $100 but Rs 5,650 on $10,000. A flat fee of a few hundred rupees is a big saving on the second and a worse deal on the first. Use fee bands, or aim first at larger tickets: students' fees, medical travel, Hajj, small importers.
- **A booking must not become a forward sale.** The booking should be a non-binding promise, with the real exchange made hand to hand at the counter. The mufti should confirm this structure.

## 10. Where to start

Start with the licence barrier and the benchmark together: become the rate rule, the receipt system and the Shariah standard for exchange companies that are already licensed. It needs no Rs 500m, it earns fee income only, and it is what the TrueRate code is already becoming. An own counter can come after that, with a known brand behind it.

## 11. Open decision

Is there access to Rs 500m in equity, or is the plan built around a licensed partner? That decides stage 3.
