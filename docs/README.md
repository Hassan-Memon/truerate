## How the ideas connect

1. **TrueRate, the site** (this repository) tells a person what a real deal cost against the market rate, and shows every source behind that answer. See the main [`README.md`](../README.md).
2. **The rate ladder** explains the answer: the published steps between the interbank rate and cash.
3. **The fee-only exchange company** would use TrueRate as its published reference rate.

The product and business ideas are kept here so that nothing is lost and can be picked up again later.
